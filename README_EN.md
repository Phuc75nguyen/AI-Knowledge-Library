## Introduction

**AI Knowledge Library** is a personal repository that consolidates academic papers, technical reports, whitepapers, and practical documentation gathered throughout ongoing study, research, and professional work in the field of Artificial Intelligence. Materials are organized by topic to support systematic reference, review, and knowledge sharing.

The repository is maintained by **Nguyen Tan Phuc**, currently working as an **AI Engineer**, with the goal of building an up-to-date reference library covering AI trends, techniques, and applications in Vietnam and around the world.

## Repository Structure

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`AI Application in TrungThuy Corp`](./AI%20Application%20in%20TrungThuy%20Corp) | Documents on applied AI in enterprise practice: real-world AI adoption experience, digital transformation strategy, and internal training materials. |
| [`Agentic AI`](./Agentic%20AI) | In-depth papers and reports on AI Agents: agent architectures, long-term memory (MemGPT), knowledge distillation, and state-of-the-industry reports (State of AI Agents). |
| [`All about ML and MLOps`](./All%20about%20ML%20and%20MLOps) | A collection of foundational and applied papers on Machine Learning Operations (MLOps): CI/CD pipelines for ML, model risk management, MLOps platform architecture, and empirical studies on operating ML systems in production. |
| [`Computer Vision`](./Computer%20Vision) | Classic papers in the field of Computer Vision, including attention mechanisms and regularization techniques for preventing overfitting in neural networks. |
| [`LLM VLLM OCR`](./LLM%20VLLM%20OCR) | Research on Large Language Models (LLM), Vision-Language Models (VLM), and Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Transformer architecture, scaling laws, reasoning, and notable models such as DeepSeek, Qwen, and PhoWhisper. |
| [`My Uni Thesis`](./My%20Uni%20Thesis) | Materials supporting the undergraduate thesis: final capstone report, the NutriAI project, a RAG-based chatbot on low-end hardware, and algorithm-review materials. |

## Purpose

- Archive and systematically organize domain knowledge accumulated through study and work.
- Provide a quick reference source for AI/ML-related projects and research.
- Share useful materials with the AI community in Vietnam and beyond.
